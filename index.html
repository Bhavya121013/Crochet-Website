⭐ The Craft Industry's Golden Pricing Formula

Many beginner crocheteers use only "Cost of Materials × 3". This works for simple goods, but drastically underpays for intricate crochet requiring 10-20 hours! Here is the industry standard formula:

Total Cost = (Yarn Cost) + (Notions/Hardware) + (Hours × Hourly Wage) + (Packaging + Overhead)

Wholesale Price = Total Cost × 1.5 to 2.0

Retail Price = Total Cost + Desired Profit Margin (Or Wholesale × 2 for boutique craft)
